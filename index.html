About us

Science-driven. Expertise-led.

BioNova Health Research is a science-driven organization founded by experts in infectious disease, immunology, molecular virology, and bioinformatics. We bring in-depth expertise in genomics and multi-omics research, supported by rigorous statistical analysis and advanced computational approaches.

Our team has collective experience as scientists within U.S. federal agencies and leading academic institutions, providing a strong foundation in rigorous study design, translational research, data quality standards, and data reporting.

We integrate transcriptomics, multi-omics, machine learning, and AI-enabled analytics to uncover biological mechanisms, identify actionable targets, and support biomarker-driven discovery and clinical development. Through close collaboration with biotech and academic partners, we transform complex data into meaningful biological insight.
