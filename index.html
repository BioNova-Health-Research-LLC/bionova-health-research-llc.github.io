About us

Science-driven. Expertise-led.

BioNova Health Research LLC is a science-driven organization founded by experts in infectious disease, immunology, molecular virology, and bioinformatics. Our team

Meet the people behind the science

Our leadership brings decades of experience spanning federal research agencies, academic medicine, and computational biology.

Maureen M. Goodenow, PhD
Founder & CEO

Dr. Goodenow is a virologist and immunologist with over three decades of research experience in HIV-1 molecular biology, viral evolution, and host-pathogen interactions. She has held senior leadership positions at the National Institutes of Health, including as Chief of the Molecular HIV Host Interactions Section at NIAID. Her work spans HIV-1 genetics, antiretroviral therapy outcomes in pediatric populations, macrophage biology, and next-generation sequencing approaches for viral analysis. With more than 120 peer-reviewed publications, she brings deep expertise in translational HIV research and federal science policy to BioNova.

John W. Sleasman, MD
Senior Advisor

Dr. Sleasman is a clinical immunologist and pediatric infectious disease specialist with a research career spanning more than three decades. He has held faculty positions at leading academic medical institutions including Duke University, where his work has focused on primary immunodeficiencies, HIV in children and adolescents, immune reconstitution, vaccine responses, and biomarker discovery. A long-standing collaborator in multi-site clinical research networks, Dr. Sleasman brings extensive expertise in study design, translational immunology, and the intersection of clinical medicine and molecular research.

Li Yin, PhD
Senior Scientist

Dr. Yin is an expert in immunology and infectious disease, with a focus on multi-omics integration, biomarker discovery, and pediatric immune development. With over two decades of experience in federal and academic settings, she has led multidisciplinary research programs that drive innovation in molecular biology and genetics.

Samiksha A. Borkar, PhD
Senior Data Analyst

Dr. Borkar is a biomedical data scientist with expertise in multi-modal transcriptomics, plasma biomarker profiling, and statistical genomics, with a focus on immune regulation in HIV and translational research.
